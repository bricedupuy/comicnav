Panels
Drag a panel to move it. Rectangle handles resize edges. Polygon vertices can be dragged; double-click an edge to add a point and double-click a vertex to remove it. Drag rows by ⋮⋮ (or use ↑/↓) to correct reading order. Hover a panel to reveal its boundary. Press Space to start Preview.
