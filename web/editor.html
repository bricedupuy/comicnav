Panels
Drag a panel to move it. Rectangle handles resize edges. Polygon vertices can be dragged; double-click an edge to add a point and double-click a vertex to remove it. Drag rows by ⋮⋮ (or use ↑/↓) to correct reading order. Hover a panel to reveal its boundary. Press Space to start Preview. Press V to validate, X to flag analysis for changes, or C to clear the page. Undo with Ctrl/Cmd+Z; redo with Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z or Ctrl/Cmd+Y.
